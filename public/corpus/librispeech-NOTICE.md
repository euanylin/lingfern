# LibriSpeech recordings

Recordings are from the [LibriSpeech ASR corpus](https://www.openslr.org/12/), prepared by Vassil Panayotov with the assistance of Daniel Povey, derived from LibriVox audiobooks and distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The accompanying licence is retained in every relevant topic package. Each recording retains the actual LibriVox reader's name and source attribution.

Changes: original continuous utterances were converted from FLAC to mono MP3 during cloud or local preparation, as recorded for each source. Original wording and punctuation are retained from the exact matched Project Gutenberg edition; print line breaks may be reflowed with an exact source-offset map. Automatic ASR supplies timing diagnostics, not replacement text. Recording IDs, source/audio hashes, exact source spans and exclusions are retained in the preparation evidence. No separate quotations or recordings are joined.

Underlying text has separate rights: the admitted editions have unrestricted U.S. text terms, with the full Project Gutenberg licence retained in each package. This does not assert worldwide clearance. Proprietary translations, unidentified translators and unresolved soundtrack/third-party voices remain withheld. Canonical original-work roots are shared across volumes; they are not independent application roots.

Practice uses the existing rule-based generator. Audio is playable and automatically checked; full auditory, physical-device and calibrated-language-level acceptance is not claimed. This data is not an authored exercise bank.
