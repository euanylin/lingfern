## Retained safety boundaries

These are standing constraints, not pending defects. They protect data or identify evidence that has not been observed.

- **TREE-PP-ATTACHMENT (scope excluded by the maintainer, 2026-11-16):** A PP after a verb and object keeps the existing VP attachment; one before the verb keeps nominal attachment. Multiple context-dependent readings are retained as a known limitation. No contextual disambiguation work or extra acceptance gate is requested. The algorithm is not claimed to resolve those readings.

- **TREE-ANALYSIS-JUDGEMENTS:** The maintainer excludes context-dependent ambiguity from current repair work. The following existing deterministic defaults remain documented choices, not validated linguistic conclusions. A PP after the verb and its object attaches to the `VP` (see `TREE-PP-ATTACHMENT` for the residual ambiguity). After `be`, a participle whose adjective senses are a clear majority of its entry is the copula's complement (`they were tired`, `the window was broken`), while one with a minority adjective reading is the passive (`the city was founded` → `nsubj:pass`); both readings are internally consistent, and the threshold is stated in `tree-parse.ts` rather than tuned per sentence. Phrase-view traces keep their coindices. Basic dependency conversion now removes traces/coindices and attaches the overt wh-filler directly to its predicate; it does not represent enhanced dependencies. No item is presented as a validated parse or a CEFR judgement.
- **TREE-OFFLINE-LEXICON:** `src/shared/tree-lexicon.ts` is generated by `npm run tree:lexicon` from the installed Open English WordNet buckets and checked for staleness by `tests/tree-lexicon.test.ts`, so it is not a hand-maintained list. The dominant lists retain the at-least-three-sense rule; a separate generated set retains all verb readings for contextual fallback, preserving noun alternatives and requiring verbal context for minority readings; grammatical words (determiners, prepositions, auxiliaries, degree adverbs) are closed-class entries in the tagger instead. Regenerate it when the dictionary changes; never extend it by hand to satisfy one sentence.
- **SOURCE-HOLDS:** Unchanged collection-time rights records remain valid evidence, but work/edition/contributor/territorial/audio/model/context holds are not bulk approvals. Keep exact exclusions and provenance in [corpus records](content/corpus/README.md); investigate changed sources or specific conflicting evidence.

User profile ZIPs are unencrypted and do not include installed media/resources. Source-backup deletion does not authorize deleting learner exports or real browser/provider data. Keep the [backup tool and recovery instructions](docs/project-backups.md).

Its historical note says an original download existed but supplies no exact path, so a complete second copy could not be confirmed.
