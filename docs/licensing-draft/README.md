The archived [Apache text](Apache-2.0.txt), [CC BY text](CC-BY-4.0.txt), [NOTICE proposal](NOTICE.proposed.txt) and [brand proposal](BRAND.proposed.md) retain their original bytes. A historical proposal does not override adopted terms.

Third-party code, texts, recordings, dictionaries, models and source-specific exclusions retain their own terms. Preserve [content notices](../../public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [dependency notices](../../Dependency_Notices.md). Reuse must identify the actual author and applicable licence; attributing Lingfern alone cannot replace third-party credits. No licence grants permission to publish private learning data, credentials or research records.
